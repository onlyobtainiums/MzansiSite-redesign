# MzansiSite — Legal & Platform Compliance Audit
### Branch `arena/82954c68-mzansisite-redesign` • Commit `d95e19f` • Audit date: 2026-10-19
### Auditor context: Codebase inspection only (no live DB, no production env secrets, no external pen-test)
### Disclaimer: This is not legal advice. Do not rely on this document as proof of compliance. Items marked “Requires attorney/privacy professional review” must be cleared by a South African attorney and, for POPIA, the Information Officer.

**Scope:** Source in `/src` (Next.js 16, Firebase Auth, Postgres/Drizzle, Paystack, Cloudinary), `src/templates/*`, `src/lib/*`, `src/db/schema.ts`, `src/proxy.ts`, `src/app/api/*`, `src/app/(app)/*`, `src/components/*`, `public/`, `.env.example`. “Deployed public pages” were reviewed via code that produces `mzansisite.co.za` and `*.mzansisite.co.za` (proxy → `/sites/[slug]`), not via live HTTP fetch (outbound hosts limited in this environment). No customer PII was accessed.

**Method:** Manual file review, grep for privacy/terms/ECTA/CPA/Paystack/ECTA s43 strings, comparison of displayed prices vs `src/lib/billing.ts` and Paystack initialize/verify/webhook, authZ review, image/font/licence audit, AI/content-policy review, OWASP Top 10 light touch, WCAG 2.2 AA gap analysis. Legislation checked against Government Gazette / Acts Online and Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za) as of 2026-10-19. Other jurisdictions (US/Firebase, Cloudinary, Paystack TOS) noted only where provider is US/NG/ZA and data/processing crosses borders.

**Rating scale:** **Critical** = block launch / immediate legal exposure; **High** = likely regulatory breach or financial loss; **Medium** = probable finding if regulator investigates; **Low** = best-practice gap; **Info** = note.

## 7. ACCESSIBILITY & USABILITY

**Legal binding vs recommendation:** South Africa has no binding WCAG law for private e-commerce (unlike US ADA, EU EAA 2025). **Promotion of Equality and Prevention of Unfair Discrimination Act** does not mandate WCAG, but **ECTA s43 + CPA s22** plain language + **B-BBEE ICT sector code** and government procurement (SITA) encourage WCAG 2.1 AA.
